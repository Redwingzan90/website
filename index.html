How the sale actually works

There is no bank in this transaction. We own the land, we sell it to you, and we carry the note ourselves — which is the only reason "no credit check" is a fact rather than a slogan.

Pick the lot, not a brochure

Every lot is identified and tracked separately. What the schedule shows as open is open; what it shows as conveyed is sold and stays visible so you can see how a property is moving.

Drive out and stand on it

Most instruments carry written directions from I-10 and the corner markers to look for — usually white plastic pipe. Call for a pin drop and go walk it before you commit to anything.

No credit check, no qualifying

We do not pull credit, verify income, or send you to a lender. Your history is not part of this. The down payment and the monthly payment are the whole conversation.

Sign, and close through title

Closings are handled through Pioneer Title Agency in Maricopa. You sign the owner-financing agreement, pay the down payment, and the conveyance is recorded with the county.

Pay it off whenever you like

No pre-payment penalty on any property, ever. Pay to the schedule for the full term or clear it early — the payoff figure is the same either way.

What you can check yourself

Buying land from a stranger on the internet deserves suspicion. So rather than ask you to take our word for anything, here is what you can independently verify before you send a dollar.

Closing
Pioneer Title Agency, Maricopa An independent title company handles the closing and the recording — not us. Call them.
Parcels
Recorded numbers, public record Parcel numbers are listed on the instruments that have them. Look any of them up with the Maricopa County Assessor.
The note
Carried by the seller KORR holds the note directly. There is no lender, no broker and no third party to qualify with.
Prepayment
No penalty, any property Stated on every instrument, and written into the agreement you sign.
