Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

This policy describes what this website collects, why, and who receives it. It covers korrbuildingcollc.netlify.app only. It does not cover what happens after you become a buyer — the loan and closing paperwork you sign has its own terms.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form, and only when you submit it:

  • Your name and telephone number — required, because a callback is the point of the form
  • Email address — optional
  • The property you asked about, and anything you write in the message box

We do not ask for, and you should never send us, a Social Security number, date of birth, bank account or card number, or a copy of an ID through this website. We do not run a credit check, so we have no reason to collect any of it.

What we do not collect

This site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and does not track you across other websites. There is no login and no account.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry about land — by phone, or by email if you gave one. That is the only purpose. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not share it with other land sellers, lead brokers, or data resellers.

Who it passes through

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the form to our email. Your submission passes through their service.
  • Google Fonts serves two typefaces. Loading them tells Google your IP address, as any web request does. No fonts are personalised and no account is involved.
  • Netlify hosts the site and keeps standard server logs.
  • Pioneer Title Agency receives your details only if you go to closing — not from this form.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

How long we keep it

Enquiries arrive as email and stay in our mailbox. If you would like yours deleted, ask and we will remove it.

Advertising

We may run advertising on Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok. If we place an advertising pixel on this site, those platforms will set cookies and may build an advertising profile from your visit, subject to their own policies. No advertising pixel is installed as of the date above. This paragraph will be updated, and a cookie notice added, before one is.

We advertise land and homes. We do not target advertising by race, colour, religion, sex, familial status, national origin, disability, or any other protected class, and we use the housing advertising category the platforms require.

Your choices

Ask us and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. Depending on where you live you may have a formal right to some of these. Either way, ask and we will do it.

This site is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Fair housing

We sell to qualified buyers without regard to race, colour, religion, sex, handicap, familial status, or national origin.
